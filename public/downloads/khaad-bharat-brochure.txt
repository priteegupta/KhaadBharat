Biochar for Bharat and Beyond

What it is:
KHAAD BHARAT Biochar is a farmer-first organic input made from bio-waste sources such as kitchen waste, agricultural residue, and dung-based materials.

Why it matters:
- Supports soil health
- Helps with water retention
- Fits farms, gardens, nurseries, and farmhouses
